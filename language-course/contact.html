FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LinguaFlow really free to start?
Yes! Our Free plan gives you access to 1 language with 10 lessons per month, basic exercises, and community access — no credit card required. You can upgrade anytime.
How long does it take to learn a language on LinguaFlow?
It depends on the language and your effort. Most learners reach conversational level (B1) in 3–6 months with daily 30-minute sessions. Our AI adapts to your pace to maximize efficiency.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime?
Absolutely. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period — no questions asked.
Are the certificates recognized internationally?
Our certificates are aligned with the CEFR framework (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages), which is recognized worldwide by universities and employers.
How do I become a tutor on LinguaFlow?
We're always looking for passionate native speakers and certified language teachers. Apply via the "Become a Tutor" option in the contact form above and we'll review your application within 5 business days.
Is LinguaFlow available on mobile?
Yes! Our iOS and Android apps are available for free. You can download lessons for offline study, practice vocabulary on the go, and maintain your daily learning streaks from anywhere.
